Italian Cream Soda


Description

A watermelon and passion fruit flavored Italian cream soda.

Ingredients

  • 8 fluid ounces carbonated water
  • ¾ fluid ounce passion fruit flavored syrup
  • ¾ fluid ounce watermelon flavored syrup
  • 1 fluid ounce half-and-half cream

Steps

  1. Fill a tall glass half full with ice.
  2. Fill to 2/3 with carbonated water.
  3. Pour in watermelon and passion fruit flavored syrups.
  4. Then float the half-and-half cream on top.
  5. Stir when ready to drink.
